Slope Is a Rate

Math 2201, Unit 6: Proportional Reasoning (indicators 6.15, 6.18, 6.19)

On a distance and time graph, the steepness of each segment is a rate: change in distance divided by change in time. Practise reading rates off a graph, then draw a graph from a described rate. This is practice only, and nothing you enter leaves this page.

Choose a segment with the buttons above, from the list, or by selecting its line on the graph. The table below gives the same information as the graph, in numbers.

Steepness is speed. A flat segment means the distance is not changing, so the rate is zero.
